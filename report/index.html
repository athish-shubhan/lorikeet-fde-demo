Wattle Money Concierge

An FDE-style proof of work: a policy-bound AI support concierge for a fictional neobank, with an eval suite, simulated customers and a shadow-replay discovery study.

Mock data. The customer, accounts, policy and ticket export are invented. Results come from an offline deterministic model, not an LLM.
